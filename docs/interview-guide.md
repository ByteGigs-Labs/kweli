# Freelancer Interview Guide (tasks M-04 / F-01)

**Goal:** 6 interviews (3 each) with Kenyan freelancers paid from abroad. Calls take 15 minutes. **Ask permission before quoting anyone.** Save anonymised notes as `docs/interviews/NN.md`, with no names, emails or phone numbers.

## Opening (read out)
"We're building a free tool that helps freelancers keep a clean record of every payment, so it's easy to answer if PayPal ever asks for documents. We'd love 15 minutes of your experience. We won't share your name unless you say yes."

## Questions
1. What work do you do, and roughly how many clients pay you from abroad each month?
2. How do clients pay you? (PayPal invoice, "send money", F&F, Payoneer, Wise, other)
3. How do you agree on work with a new client? (WhatsApp, email, platform, written contract?)
4. Has PayPal ever limited your account or asked for documents? When?
5. What exactly did they ask for? (ID, proof of address, contracts, invoices, bank statements, other)
6. Which of those did you have? Which were hard to produce, and why?
7. How much money was held, for how long, and what happened in the end?
8. Have you had a client open a dispute or chargeback? What happened?
9. If a tool kept every agreement, invoice and deliverable linked to each payment, would you use it? What would make you trust it?
10. Would you pay for it? What feels fair per month (KES or USD), and would you pay through PayPal?

## Close
- "Can we quote you anonymously, e.g. 'a Nairobi graphic designer'?" Record a yes or no.
- "Can we show you the prototype next week (11 Nov) for 10 minutes?"
